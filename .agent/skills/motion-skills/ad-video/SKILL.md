# 🎯 Performance Video Ad Skills (iart-ai Pack)

This skill governs **high-converting video advertisements, commercial hooks, and retention engineering**.

---

## 🏆 The 4-Beat Commercial Structure

1. **Beat 1: The 3-Second Visual Hook (Frames 0–90):**
   - High visual contrast or unexpected statement.
   - Immediate audio trigger: low sub-bass boom or crisp whoosh.
   - Hero badge or problem statement prominently displayed within Title Safe zone (top 20%–40%).

2. **Beat 2: The Core Value Proposition (Frames 90–300):**
   - Introduce the institution, product, or solution.
   - Use clean, structured cards (e.g. flexible schedules, career advancement, B.Tech lateral entry).

3. **Beat 3: Social Proof & Quantitative Evidence (Frames 300–600):**
   - Concrete metrics: 30 seats per specialization, AICTE accreditation seal, placement statistics.
   - Use animated charts or 3D technical models (Crucible 3D gears/transformers) to elevate authority.

4. **Beat 4: Urgency & Unmistakable CTA (Final 90–150 Frames):**
   - Clear deadline or limited intake notice.
   - Direct helpline numbers (`9434002626 / 9434007137`) and web portal URL.
   - Bouncing CTA pill badge with persistent glow.

## 📱 Multi-Aspect Ratio Conversion Matrix

| Dimension | 16:9 Landscape (YouTube / Desktop / TV) | 9:16 Vertical (Reels / Shorts / TikTok) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hero Focus** | Centered or 2-column comparison layout | Stacked vertical card sequence |
| **Top Margin** | 10% from top edge | **15% Top Danger Zone** (clears creator name & search icon) |

| **Typography** | Wider horizontal lines (4–8 words) | Compact, punchy lines (2–4 words per line) |
